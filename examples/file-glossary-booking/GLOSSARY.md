# Booking Glossary

Terms for a minimal hotel booking domain.

## People and places

| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Guest | A person making a **room** reservation. | Customer, Client |
| Room | A bookable hotel room. | |

## Reservations

| Term | Meaning | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| book | Reserve a **Room** for a given stay. | |
| Confirmation | A notification acknowledging a successful reservation. | Receipt |
| Cancellation Policy | The rules governing *how* and *when* a booking may be cancelled.<br>Applies to every **Room** rate, such as `flexible` or `non-refundable`. | Refund Rules (in partner contracts only) |
| Overbooking | Accepting more reservations than there are **rooms** available. | |
